How Consumers works

Thirty days, one workflow.

No prerequisites and no code. The scoping call is the door — we map a workflow worth rebuilding before you commit, then you ship it inside your cohort.

1

Scope

Book a scoping call. We pick one workflow your team runs every week — the one that costs the most time.

2

Rebuild

Over 30 days on Orion, rebuild it with AI inside the tools you already use — no new stack to learn.

3

Prove

Measure the ROI — hours saved, error rate, throughput — and ship it to production with an optional 2-hr pilot.
