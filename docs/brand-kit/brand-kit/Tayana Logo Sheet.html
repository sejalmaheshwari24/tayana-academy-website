What it meansRead the mark

The mortarboard names the institution — an academy, not a bootcamp. The Sun tassel is the credential, the one spark of Sun reserved for the moment of earning. The three bars are the architecture: Indigo for Builders and Spring for Consumers rising on either side of the Ink anchor — two pillars, one academy. At favicon scale the tassel drops and the cap plus bars still read.
