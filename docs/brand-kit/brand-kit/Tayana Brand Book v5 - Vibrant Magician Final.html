02 · The Brief

What changed from v3.2.

v4 is a direction reset, not a structural rebuild. The 23-section brand book skeleton is intact. The voice, palette, and visual register move from the warm-editorial Sage × Mentor archetype to the vibrant multi-color Magician archetype, locked from team feedback.

This book carries forward: 20-section site structure · Builders & Consumers pillar architecture · 24-cohorts-per-year cadence · trade-secret-protected content model · Inter + JetBrains Mono + Instrument Serif type stack · the strict no-AI-suffix rule · WCAG AA targets.
This book replaces: warm Foundry Materials palette (Iron/Copper/Brass) → vibrant Magician palette (Indigo/Spring/Sun/Coral on cream + Ink) · editorial-restrained voice → vibrant outcome-led voice · monochrome-medal logo lock → pending logo direction (interim mark stays).

Why we moved

Consolidated team feedback called the v3.2 direction "dull, old-fashioned, and not academy enough for a modern AI institution." The team picked Direction A · Vibrant Magician from the Palette Triangle. Vishal noted a personal preference for Direction C (Magician × Sage Hybrid) — captured in open items, can swap palette tokens in if Sharad lands there.

This book ships as the production reference for Direction A. The structure is portable — if Sharad locks C later, the token swap is a 30-minute job, not a rebuild.

What's locked in v5

Palette. Direction A · Vibrant Magician on light mode is confirmed as the production direction. Logo. The interim wordmark and retired Circle Medal are replaced by the locked symbol-led mark (§06). Surface material. Liquid glass is adopted as the site's signature chrome (§14). Remaining open items — icon library commission and photography shoot — are tracked in the companion status doc.

10 · Voice & Tone

Direct. Outcome-led. Alive.

Magician archetype voice: vivid, transformation-led, specific. Speaks like a working operator describing real outcomes — not a recruiter pitching aspirations. Energy comes from precision, not exclamation.

Vocabulary chips · what we say

ShipEngineerOutcome WorkflowApplyRole CohortRegisterPilot VerifiedProductionNow

Vocabulary blocks · what we don't say

Transform Journey Bootcamp Limited seats! Unlock Game-changing Empower Best-in-class

Do · don't

Do
  • Lead with outcomes. "73% reduction in analyst prep time" not "improve productivity"
  • Name the cohort. "Builders Cohort 09" not "our latest batch"
  • Use the second person sparingly. Specificity > you-language
  • Let proof do the persuasion. Numbers · alumni names with consent · pilot results
  • End sentences with periods. Periods are confidence
  • Pick one Instrument Serif italic span per heading. One brand moment, not three
Don't
  • Use exclamation marks. Ever. Energy is in precision, not punctuation
  • Promise "transformation." Promise outcomes
  • Use "bootcamp." Tayana runs cohorts, not bootcamps
  • Manufacture urgency. Cadence is the urgency (1st & 15th)
  • Stack adjectives. "World-class, cutting-edge, transformative" → nothing
  • Use gradients in display copy. One color span per heading, not two

14 · Surface Material · Liquid Glass

One material. Liquid glass.

The signature surface for the site. Floating chrome — the nav, cohort cards over photography, the masterclass modal, the pricing rail — is rendered as translucent, blurred "liquid glass": it refracts the vibrant colour behind it instead of covering it. Content on a plain page stays flat and opaque. Glass is chrome, never a reading surface.

Real backdrop blur plus saturation lifts the Indigo/Spring/Sun palette straight through the panel, so the brand's colour is always alive at the edges. A thin top-lit border and a soft specular sheen sell the glass; a hairline shadow floats it off the page.

The recipe

.glass {
  background: rgba(255,255,255, .14);  /* light mode: white veil */
  backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%);
  -webkit-backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%);
  border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255, .28);  /* top-lit edge */
  box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255,255,255,.45), /* specular sheen */
            0 12px 40px rgba(14,14,20,.24); /* float */
  border-radius: 14px;
}

Tokens: blur 20px chrome / 24px cards / 30px modals · saturate 180% · veil white 12–18% on colour, Ink 40–55% when glass sits over light content · edge 1px white 28% · sheen inset 1px white 45% · float shadow 0 12px 40px Ink/24%. Always ship a solid fallback colour for browsers without backdrop-filter.

Do. Use glass for floating chrome over colour or photography — nav, modals, cohort cards, the sticky enroll rail, toasts. Keep text on glass white or Ink at ≥AA. One glass layer at a time.
Don't. Never stack glass on glass, never set long-form reading (LMS body, articles) on glass, never blur behind text a user is actively reading, and never drop the material below 4.5:1 contrast. No coloured tint in the glass itself — colour comes from behind it.

17 · Photography

Real learners. Real outcomes.

No stock people. No abstract AI substrate art. No mountains. Photography is real learners, real cohort sessions, real mentor demos, real before/after of shipped projects.

Direction

  • Real learners in their actual contexts — at the desk, on the Orion call, presenting a project. Wide warm light. No studio gloss.
  • Cohort sessions photographed in action — laptops open, sticky notes on the wall, mentor at the whiteboard.
  • Before/after of shipped work — screenshot of the broken process + screenshot of the agent that fixed it. The transformation IS the photography.
  • Mentor demos — close-up on the screen + ambient mentor in soft focus.
  • Pillar color overlays at low opacity — Indigo gradient on Builders shots, Spring on Consumers shots. Brand cohesion without lazy filters.
Forbidden: stock photos of "diverse young professionals smiling at laptops" · AI-generated learner photos · abstract circuit board / mountain / network diagrams · anyone wearing a hoodie in a parking garage · women looking at men's screens.

20 · Motion Principles

Transformation, not decoration.

Magician archetype motion: before-after, scroll-driven storytelling, reveal-on-intersect. Every animation earns its presence by showing a state change — not by moving for sake of moving.

Timing curves

  • 180ms · cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1) — hover states, button presses, link transitions.
  • 320ms · cubic-bezier(.2, .7, .2, 1) — section reveals, accordion opens, card lifts.
  • 520ms · cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1) — page transitions, hero load-in, count-up animations.
  • 800ms · linear-ish — marquees, infinite tickers, cohort calendar reveals.

Approved motion patterns

  • Scroll-reveal stagger · IntersectionObserver → opacity 0→1, translateY 12px → 0, 320ms, 50ms stagger between siblings.
  • Count-up · stat band numerals animate 0 → value on scroll-into-view, 520ms ease-out cubic.
  • Marquee · horizontal infinite · linear, pause on hover for testimonial ticker.
  • Hero gradient drift · slow radial-gradient parallax behind hero text (Indigo + Spring blobs · 60s loop · low intensity).
  • Before/after slider · drag a vertical handle to reveal "before AI workflow" vs "after AI workflow" screenshots in success story sections.
  • FAQ accordion · max-height transition, 320ms, line-cross icon rotation.
Forbidden: spinning logos · text bouncing on entrance · parallax that distracts from reading · ANY animation in the LMS body reading region · motion behind text the user is trying to read.

22 · Sub-brand Rules

One brand. No sub-brands.

Tayana Academy is one brand. Builders and Consumers are pillars, not sub-brands. Cohort themes (Distributed Systems, Financial Operations) are presentation, not brand. Orion (LMS) and Nebula (CRM) are internal product names, not customer-facing brands.

Rules

  • No "Tayana Academy Pro / Premium / Plus." Cohort access is uniform per pillar.
  • No "Tayana Academy India / US / UK" as visible brand variants. Geography is a content layer · same logo · same palette.
  • Pillar names always read as "Tayana Academy · Builders" / "Tayana Academy · Consumers" — never standalone.
  • Cohort badges are content chips, not brand marks. Format: "Cohort 09 · Builders · Spring 2026."
  • Partnership co-brands (e.g., curriculum aligned with OpenAI) are content credits, not sub-brands. Never lock-up a partner logo with the Tayana mark.

24 · Forbidden Patterns

Things that break the brand.

Hard rules. No exceptions without brand-owner amendment.

Logo

  • Never add "AI" to the master mark · ever.
  • Never combine the wordmark with another brand's logo in a single lock-up.
  • Never apply gradient, drop shadow, 3D, or beveled effects to the mark.

Color

  • Never use saturated bright accent as long-form background. Vibrant lives in moments, not surfaces.
  • Never use Indigo and Spring directly adjacent in UI (vibrates · always separate with neutral or Ink).
  • Never use Sun on white as text (fails AA).
  • Never use pillar accents as body text on white. Pillar accents are UI moments.

Type

  • Never use Instrument Serif in body copy. Display H1/H2 emphasis spans only.
  • Never use exclamation marks in marketing copy. Energy is in precision.
  • Never use ALL CAPS on H1/H2. Capitalize. Uppercase is for labels (Mono) only.

Tone

  • Never promise "transformation." Promise outcomes.
  • Never use the word "bootcamp."
  • Never use "limited seats" scarcity manufactured urgency. Cadence is the urgency.
  • Never fabricate quotes from real public figures (Sam Altman, Sundar Pichai, etc.). Verified public quotes with citation only.

Architecture

  • Never create a "Tayana Academy Pro / Premium" sub-brand. One brand.
  • Never let pillar cohorts share visual identity (Builders = Indigo · Consumers = Spring · pillar-exclusive).
  • Never reference parent businesses or holding entities on customer-facing surfaces.
If a brief asks for something on this list, escalate to brand owner before producing. This book is canonical. No exceptions without brand-owner amendment and version bump.
