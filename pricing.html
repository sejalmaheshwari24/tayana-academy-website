Questions

Still wondering if this is for you?

Is the masterclass really free?

Yes. The masterclass is the door — you see the room, meet mentors and screen your fit before any commitment or payment.

What if I need Python or Full-Stack first?

Foundation courses are included in the Builders track. If screening routes you to Python Foundation or Full-Stack Foundation, you take it and re-assess at no extra cost.

Can I switch pillars?

Pillars are exclusive per cohort. If your goals change, you can join the other pillar's next cohort — talk to us and we'll help you pick.

Do you offer refunds?

Because seats are limited (24 Builders / 20 Consumers), refund windows close before each cohort start. The masterclass exists so you can decide before you pay.
